Founder notes

Reality check

What has to be true

  • Homeowners with glass front doors will pay your kit price for on-demand privacy (vs blinds, static frost, or leaving it clear).
  • Cut-to-fit + remote install is DIY-safe enough that returns and support do not eat the margin (power near the door, clean adhesion, no peel/yellow).
  • Your offer beats commodity Amazon PDLC kits on fit, trust, or install help—not on “instant fog with a remote,” which already exists.

Strongest evidence for

  • Real privacy tension: glass front doors expose the entry to the street/porch.
  • Category works: consumer PDLC / switchable smart film kits with remotes already sell on Amazon (door/window sizes often ~$300–$500+).

Strongest evidence against

  • You are entering a commodity DIY category, not inventing the effect.
  • Front-door installs are awkward (power, irregular glass, sidelights, sun/heat, guest-facing failure modes).
  • Cheap substitutes exist: curtains, static privacy film, frosted glass—no wiring.

Biggest assumption to test first

  • A real homeowner with a glass front door will prepay your kit (at your price) instead of buying an Amazon PDLC kit or a non-electric privacy fix.

Founder watch-out

  • If the landing page sells “magic fog remote,” you look like every other kit; win only if you own front-door fit, install confidence, and warranty—not the fog effect.
